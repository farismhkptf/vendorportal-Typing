2. Employment Types
Permanent Staff (memberType: "permanent")
Full-time employees of your organization
Can be assigned as default staff for their role
Can go on leave with coverage assignment
Assigned to companies as Client Relationship Manager or Medical PRO
When on leave, temporary staff covers their responsibilities
Temporary Staff (memberType: "temporary")
Contract or temporary workers
Cannot be set as default staff for a role
Primary purpose: cover for permanent staff on leave
Linked via coveringForId to the permanent staff they're replacing
Shown in coverage selection dropdown when marking someone on leave

4. Status System
Active Status (status: "active")
Currently working
Appears in assignment dropdowns
Handles their assigned work
On Leave Status (status: "on_leave")
Temporarily unavailable
Has a leaveEndDate showing expected return
Must have coverage assigned (temporary staff)
Badge shows "On Leave (until DD MMM)"
5. Leave Management Flow
Marking Staff as On Leave
When a permanent staff member goes on leave:

Click the status button (Active badge)
Select coverage - Choose from list of temporary staff members
Set leave end date - When they're expected back
Confirm - Updates status to "on_leave"
toggleStatusMutation.mutate({ 
  id: member.id, 
  status: 'on_leave', 
  leaveEndDate: selectedDate,      // When leave ends
  coveringForId: temporaryStaffId  // Who covers
});
Setting Staff as Active
When staff returns from leave:

Click the status button (On Leave badge)
Click "Set as Active"
Status updates - Clears leave date and coverage assignment
toggleStatusMutation.mutate({ 
  id: member.id, 
  status: 'active', 
  leaveEndDate: null,     // Clear leave date
  coveringForId: null     // Clear coverage
});

7. Default Staff Designation
The isDefault flag marks one staff member as the default for their role:

isDefault: boolean("is_default").default(false).notNull()
Use Case: When auto-assigning work or creating new companies, the system can pick the default staff for that role.

Business Rule: Only ONE person per role should be marked as default (enforced in UI with a star icon toggle).
