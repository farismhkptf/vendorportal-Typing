# Second-Pass Audit — Product Purpose, Human Logic & Module Value

For every major module/page/flow, please answer:

1. Why does this module exist?
2. Who is it mainly for?
3. What job is the user supposed to complete here?
4. Is the module currently serving that purpose?
5. What would a human user find confusing, incomplete, illogical, or risky?
6. What is missing for the module to become genuinely useful?
7. What is unnecessary, duplicated, or premature?
8. What should be improved now?
9. What should be held for later?
10. What needs product clarification before implementation?

Please cover at least:

- Internal dashboard
- Work orders
- New work order creation
- Quick paste parser
- Typing jobs
- Medical scheduling
- EID / biometrics scheduling
- Vendor portal
- Vendor V2 portal
- Attestation vendor portal
- Admin console
- Reports
- Document custody
- Attestation inquiries and quotes
- Settings / system health
- Notifications
- Login / role experience
- Mobile experience where inferable
