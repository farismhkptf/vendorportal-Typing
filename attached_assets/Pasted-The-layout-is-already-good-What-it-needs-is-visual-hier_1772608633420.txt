The layout is already good. What it needs is visual hierarchy improvements so the eye moves naturally when someone opens the email on a phone. Right now everything is readable, but nothing is commanding attention.

Here are the most effective UI improvements ranked by impact.

⸻

1 — Turn Date & Time into a “Priority Card” ⭐ (Most Important)

Right now date and time sit quietly inside the table.

In appointment emails the single most important information is when to show up.

Make this section visually dominant.

Current feeling
Small boxes inside the table → easy to miss.

Better approach

Appointment Schedule

┌───────────────────────┬───────────────────┐
│        DATE           │       TIME        │
│                       │                   │
│     26 February       │      6:00 PM      │
│        2026           │                   │
└───────────────────────┴───────────────────┘

UI improvements

• slightly larger card
• stronger background tint
• bold typography
• centered content

This becomes the visual anchor of the email.

⸻

2 — Split Information into Clean Sections

Right now the Appointment Details block is dense.

Split into two logical groups:

A. Application Details

Work Order
Company
Applicant

B. Appointment Location

Center

Map

This removes cognitive overload.

3 — Make the Map Link a Button

Right now it looks like plain text.

Instead of:

Google Maps

[ Open Location in Google Maps ]

People open this email while traveling to the center.
Make navigation one tap obvious.

The Core Principle

Appointment emails should follow this visual reading order:

1️⃣ Title
2️⃣ Date & Time
3️⃣ Location
4️⃣ Applicant
5️⃣ Contact person
6️⃣ Notes

Right now Date/Time is buried.
Bring it forward and the entire email becomes clearer.
