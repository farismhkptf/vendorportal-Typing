#111 — Quick Paste Parser Safety Fix (Company Not Found Handling)

## What & Why

The Quick Paste parser currently treats company matching too aggressively.

Current failure:
When a pasted company name does not exist in the system, the parser still selects the nearest fuzzy match and presents it as a valid company.

This is unsafe.

A Work Order must never attach to the wrong company because of a low-confidence guess.

The parser must move from **guessing behavior** to **confidence-based decision behavior**.

Before implementing:
Please audit the current parser and challenge this plan if there is a smaller, safer, or better approach based on the actual codebase.

## Core rule

If the company does not exist in the system:

* do not auto-match another company
* do not auto-fill company field
* do not silently substitute

Unknown must remain unknown.

## Done looks like

### High confidence match

If confidence is strong:

* company is auto-detected
* company is applied normally

### Medium confidence match

If confidence is uncertain:

* system shows possible matches
* user must explicitly choose one
* nothing auto-applies

### Low confidence / no match

If confidence is weak:

* no company is selected
* original pasted company text remains visible
* visible warning appears:
  "Company not found in system"

### Apply to Form protection

If company is unresolved:

* company field remains empty
* Apply to Form must not inject a wrong company

Other fields may still apply normally if valid.

## Additional parser improvement

Review applicant name detection:

Current issue:
Long names may be skipped and job titles may be detected as names.

Please review and improve safely.

Suggested direction:

* allow up to 7-word names
* improve exclusion list for job-title words
* prefer positional parsing where safer (after company, before service/status)

Challenge this if there is a better code-aware approach.

## Out of scope

* Auto-creating companies
* Changing database schema
* Service type parser redesign
* Workflow changes outside Quick Paste
* Changing Work Order creation flow
